Primates

Mountain gorilla trekking

Few wildlife encounters on earth compare to an hour in the company of a mountain gorilla family. With only around 1,060 left in the wild, every permit directly funds their protection.

Small groups of no more than eight visitors set off with expert rangers and trackers, following the family's trail through bamboo and dense montane forest. When you find them, you spend one unforgettable hour watching silverbacks, mothers and playful infants just metres away.

Where
Bwindi Impenetrable & Mgahinga (Uganda); Volcanoes National Park (Rwanda)
Best time
Year-round. Driest trails Jun–Sep & Dec–Feb
Cost guide
Permit: Uganda $800 · Rwanda $1,500 · Uganda 4-hour habituation $1,500
Fitness
Challenging Moderate to challenging — 1 to 6 hours of hiking at 1,600–2,600 m. Porters available ($20). Minimum age 15.

Primates

Chimpanzee tracking

Loud, sociable and astonishingly human, chimpanzees share 98.7% of our DNA. Tracking them through the forest — hooting calls echoing around you — is pure adrenaline.

Kibale Forest is the primate capital of East Africa, with around 1,500 chimpanzees and a sighting success rate above 90%. For a deeper experience, the full-day habituation lets you stay with a community from dawn nesting to evening nest-building.

Where
Kibale Forest, Kyambura Gorge & Budongo (Uganda); Nyungwe Forest (Rwanda)
Best time
Year-round. Best Jun–Sep & Dec–Feb when trails are dry
Cost guide
Permit: Kibale $250 · Kyambura Gorge $50 · full-day habituation also available
Fitness
Moderate Moderate — 2 to 4 hours on mostly flat forest trails; chimps move fast. Minimum age 12.

Classic safari

Big Five game drives

Lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo and rhino — the legendary five, and the cheetahs, giraffes, hippos and hyenas that share their plains.

Our private 4x4 Land Cruisers have pop-up roofs, charging points and binoculars on board, and your guide sets the pace — leave before dawn for the big cats, linger at a sighting as long as you like, and break for a bush breakfast in the shade of an acacia.

Where
Masai Mara (Kenya); Serengeti & Ngorongoro (Tanzania); Akagera (Rwanda); Murchison Falls & Queen Elizabeth (Uganda)
Best time
Jun–Oct & Jan–Feb dry seasons, when animals gather at water
Cost guide
Park fees roughly $40–$200 pp per day — always included in our safari prices
Fitness
Easy Easy — suitable for all ages. Game drives of 3–4 hours, or full days with a picnic lunch.

Wildlife spectacle

The Great Migration

Some 1.5 million wildebeest and 300,000 zebra moving in an endless loop between Tanzania and Kenya, followed every step of the way by lions, cheetahs and crocodiles.

Timing is everything. The herds calve on the southern Serengeti plains in January and February, push north through the Western Corridor around June, and from July to October make the dramatic Mara River crossings. We book mobile camps that follow the herds, so you are never far from the action.

Where
Serengeti National Park (Tanzania) and the Masai Mara (Kenya)
Best time
Jul–Oct for river crossings; Jan–Feb for calving in Ndutu
Cost guide
Peak-season camps from about $550 pp per night; our 10-day migration safari from $11,900
Fitness
Easy Easy — long game drives on bumpy tracks, but no hiking required.

1,080+ species

Birding safaris

Uganda alone has recorded more than 1,080 bird species — half of Africa's total — including the prehistoric-looking shoebill and 23 Albertine Rift endemics.

Our specialist birding guides know every call. Paddle a wooden canoe through Mabamba swamp in search of the shoebill, tick off green broadbills and Rwenzori turacos in Bwindi, and scan Lake Nakuru's shores for pink clouds of flamingos.

Where
Mabamba Wetland, Bwindi, Murchison & Queen Elizabeth (Uganda); Nyungwe (Rwanda); Lake Nakuru (Kenya)
Best time
Year-round. Nov–Apr brings Palearctic migrants and breeding plumage
Cost guide
Mabamba shoebill canoe about $60 pp; specialist birding guide from $150 per day
Fitness
Easy Easy to moderate — early starts and some forest walking on uneven ground.

Adventure

Hiking & volcano treks

From the snows of Kilimanjaro to the glaciers of the Rwenzoris and the crater lake atop Mount Bisoke, East Africa's mountains are some of the most rewarding on earth.

Whether it is a gentle walk to Sipi Falls through Mount Elgon's coffee farms, a full-day volcano hike in the Virungas, or a summit attempt on Africa's highest peak, we handle permits, guides, porters and acclimatisation so you can focus on the view.

Where
Kilimanjaro (Tanzania); Rwenzori Mountains & Sipi Falls (Uganda); Mount Bisoke & Muhabura (Rwanda)
Best time
Jan–Feb & Jun–Oct for clear skies and dry trails
Cost guide
Mount Bisoke day hike $75 · Kilimanjaro 7–8 day climbs from about $2,500 pp
Fitness
Challenging Challenging — multi-day treks at altitude. Good fitness and some hiking experience essential.

People

Culture & communities

East Africa's people are its greatest treasure. Meet them on their own terms — through music, food, craft and conversation — with community-run experiences that share income fairly.

Walk with Batwa guides through the forest that was once their home, join a Maasai warrior on a bush walk, dance at Kampala's Ndere Centre, cook in a Ugandan village kitchen, and pay your respects at the Kigali Genocide Memorial.

Where
Bwindi Batwa trail & Ndere Centre (Uganda); Maasai villages (Kenya & Tanzania); Iby'Iwacu village & Kigali (Rwanda)
Best time
Year-round
Cost guide
Most community visits $20–$60 pp — paid directly to the community
Fitness
Easy Easy — gentle walks, suitable for families and all ages.

Unwind

Zanzibar beach escapes

After the dust and early starts of safari, nothing beats warm Indian Ocean water and powder-white sand. Zanzibar is just a short flight from the Serengeti or Kilimanjaro.

Wander Stone Town's spice-scented lanes, sail a traditional dhow at sunset, snorkel with turtles at Mnemba Atoll, then retreat to a boutique beach lodge in Nungwi, Matemwe or Paje. Perfect for honeymoons and families alike.

Where
Zanzibar: Stone Town, Nungwi & Kendwa, Matemwe, Paje and Mnemba Atoll
Best time
Jun–Oct & Dec–Feb for sunshine and calm seas
Cost guide
Beach lodges from about $180 pp per night; Mnemba snorkelling trip about $60 pp
Fitness
Easy Easy — as active or as lazy as you like.

Gorilla trekking compared

Uganda vs Rwanda

Both offer world-class, life-changing gorilla encounters. The right choice depends on your budget, time and what else you'd like to see.

FeatureUganda Bwindi & MgahingaRwanda Volcanoes NP
Permit price$800$1,500
Habituated families20+ across four sectors of Bwindi, plus Mgahinga12 in Volcanoes National Park
Typical trek1–6 hours; steeper, denser forest1–4 hours; bamboo, gentler slopes
Time with gorillas1 hour (4 hours on habituation)1 hour
Getting thereFly to Entebbe, then 1h45 flight or 8–9 hr drive; or 4 hrs from Kigali2.5 hr drive from Kigali on smooth roads
Other primatesChimps in Kibale, golden monkeys in MgahingaGolden monkeys in Volcanoes, chimps in Nyungwe
LodgesWide range, from $150 to $1,500+ per nightMore luxury-led, from $250 to $3,000+ per night
Best forValue, longer itineraries, classic safari combinationsShort trips, ease of access, luxury
