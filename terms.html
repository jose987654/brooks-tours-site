Booking Terms & Conditions

The short version

  • A 30% deposit plus the full cost of any gorilla or chimpanzee permits confirms your booking.
  • The balance is due 60 days before your safari starts.
  • Permits are non-refundable once purchased, so travel insurance is compulsory.
  • Cancellation charges increase the closer you are to departure.

1. About these terms

These terms apply to every safari, tour, transfer and related service ("your trip") booked with Brooks Tours & Travels Ltd ("we", "us", "our"), a company registered in Uganda with its office at Plot 12, Kololo Hill Drive, Kampala. We are licensed by the Uganda Tourism Board and are a member of the Association of Uganda Tour Operators (AUTO).

"You" means every person named on the booking. The person who makes the booking (the "lead traveller") confirms they are authorised to accept these terms on behalf of everyone in the group and is responsible for payments.

2. Making a booking

A booking is confirmed, and a contract formed between us, when we receive your deposit and send you a written booking confirmation. Please check your confirmation carefully, including names as they appear in passports, and tell us about any errors within seven days. Errors in names may make permits invalid and corrections can incur fees.

Itineraries we send before confirmation are proposals only. Lodges, permits and flights are subject to availability until confirmed.

3. Deposits & payment

  • Deposit: 30% of the total trip price, plus 100% of the cost of any gorilla, chimpanzee or golden monkey permits, is payable at the time of booking.
  • Balance: the remaining balance is due no later than 60 days before your trip starts. Bookings made within 60 days of departure must be paid in full at booking.
  • Late payment: if the balance is not received on time, we may treat the booking as cancelled by you and apply the cancellation charges in section 6.
  • Methods: bank transfer in US dollars (you pay your own bank charges), or card payment via our secure payment link, which carries a 4% processing fee. Residents of Uganda may also pay by mobile money.

4. Prices

All prices are quoted in US dollars, per person, based on two people sharing unless stated otherwise. Our quotations state exactly what is included and excluded. International flights, visas, travel insurance, tips and personal expenses are not included unless clearly listed.

Once your trip is paid in full we will not increase the price, except where a government or park authority introduces or increases a tax, levy, park fee or permit fee. In that case we will pass on the exact increase only and show you the evidence.

5. Gorilla, chimpanzee & other permits

Permits are purchased in your name from the Uganda Wildlife Authority, the Rwanda Development Board or other park authorities as soon as your deposit is received. Once purchased, permits are non-refundable by those authorities, and therefore by us, regardless of the reason for cancellation, including illness on the day of trekking.

Park authorities may allow a permit to be rescheduled to another date under their own rules and fees. We will always try to do this for you, but cannot guarantee it. Minimum age for gorilla trekking is 15 years. Rangers may refuse entry to anyone showing signs of a contagious illness; in that case the permit is not refundable.

Trekking involves walking in steep, muddy terrain at altitude for between one and eight hours. Sightings of gorillas are very likely but, as with all wildlife, cannot be guaranteed.

6. Cancellation by you

You may cancel at any time by written notice from the lead traveller. Cancellation takes effect on the date we receive it. The following charges apply, as a percentage of the total trip price excluding permits:

Notice before trip startsCancellation charge
90 days or moreDeposit (30%)
89 to 60 days50%
59 to 30 days75%
29 days or less, or no-show100%

In every case the full cost of permits is non-refundable, and any flights we have ticketed are subject to the airline's own rules. Some lodges, especially during the peak months and around Christmas, apply stricter terms; where this is the case we will tell you before you book and the stricter terms will apply. No refunds are given for unused services once your trip has started.

7. Changes you request

If you wish to change your trip after confirmation, we will do our best to help. Each change incurs an administration fee of $50 per booking plus any costs charged by our suppliers. Changes within 60 days of departure may be treated as a cancellation and rebooking. Transferring a booking to another person is possible only where permits and suppliers allow it.

8. Changes or cancellation by us

Safaris operate in a natural environment, and occasionally we must change part of an itinerary. Minor changes, such as replacing a lodge with one of a similar standard or altering the order of activities, may be made without compensation. If we need to make a significant change, or cancel your trip, we will tell you as soon as possible and offer you a choice of an alternative trip of equal or higher standard, a lower-standard alternative with a refund of the price difference, or a full refund of all money paid to us.

We will not be liable for compensation where a change or cancellation is caused by events outside our control ("force majeure"), such as extreme weather, natural disasters, epidemics, civil unrest, government action, closure of parks or borders, or airline failures. In those cases we will refund whatever we are able to recover from our suppliers, less permits that cannot be rescheduled.

9. Travel insurance

Comprehensive travel insurance is a condition of booking. Your policy must cover, as a minimum, cancellation and curtailment (including the full value of permits), medical expenses, emergency evacuation by air, repatriation, and personal liability, and must cover the activities in your itinerary such as gorilla trekking, walking safaris and hot-air ballooning. We may ask for your policy details before travel. Zanzibar requires all visitors to hold the island's own inbound travel insurance, which we can arrange.

10. Passports, visas & health

You are responsible for holding a passport valid for at least six months beyond your return, the correct visas, and any vaccination certificates required, including a yellow fever certificate for entry to Uganda. We provide general guidance on our Plan Your Safari page, but you must check current requirements with official sources. We are not responsible if you are refused travel or entry.

Please tell us before booking about any medical condition, disability or reduced mobility that may affect your trip, so we can advise you honestly and make reasonable arrangements.

11. Your conduct

You agree to follow the instructions of your guide, park rangers and lodge staff, particularly around wildlife, and to respect the laws and customs of the countries you visit. We or our suppliers may end the trip of anyone whose behaviour endangers themselves, other people or wildlife, without refund. Drones must not be flown in national parks without official permits.

12. Our liability

We will provide your trip with reasonable skill and care. We are responsible for the services we provide directly, including our vehicles and guides. Where we arrange services provided by independent suppliers, such as lodges, airlines and park authorities, we will select them with care, but their own terms and the international conventions that govern them apply.

We are not liable for any loss, injury or damage that is caused by your own actions, by a third party unconnected with your trip, or by unforeseeable events beyond our control, including the natural behaviour of wild animals. Except in cases of death or personal injury caused by our negligence, our liability is limited to a maximum of the total price of your trip. Nothing in these terms limits any rights you have under the laws of Uganda that cannot be excluded.

13. Complaints

If something is not right during your trip, please tell your guide or call our Kampala office straight away so we can put it right on the spot. Most issues are resolved within hours. If you remain unhappy, please write to us within 28 days of returning home at hello@brookstours.co.ug. We will acknowledge your complaint within five working days and give a full response within 28 days.

If we cannot resolve your complaint, you may refer it to the Association of Uganda Tour Operators or the Uganda Tourism Board.

14. Governing law

These terms and any contract between us are governed by the laws of the Republic of Uganda. Any dispute will be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Uganda, although we will always first seek to resolve matters amicably or through mediation.
