Welcome to Brooks

Private journeys, curated around you

No group departures, no fixed coaches. Every Brooks safari is built from scratch for the people travelling, with your own vehicle, your own guide and a pace that suits you.

We are a Kampala-based team of planners and guides who have spent more than a decade on the tracks of Bwindi, Kibale, the Mara and the Serengeti. We know which lodges deliver, which permits sell out first and which valley the gorillas favour in the wet months. That knowledge is what you are really booking.

How it works

From first idea to first sunrise

One dedicated planner from your first message to your flight home. No call centres, no pressure.

  1. 01

    Tell us your dream

    Share dates, budget and the moments you are after. A short form or a WhatsApp message is enough.

  2. 02

    We design it

    Within 24 hours you receive a day-by-day itinerary with handpicked lodges and transparent pricing.

  3. 03

    Refine together

    Tweak routes, upgrade rooms, add Zanzibar. We secure permits the moment you confirm.

  4. 04

    Travel, fully looked after

    Your guide meets you at Entebbe or Kigali, and our team is on call 24/7 until you are home.
