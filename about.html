About Brooks Tours & Travels

Born in Kampala. Built by guides.

For more than a decade we have been quietly crafting private safaris across East Africa — led by people who grew up here, and who still get goosebumps at the first sound of a silverback.

Our story

From one Land Cruiser to East Africa's most personal safari house

Brooks Tours & Travels began in 2012 with a single, much-loved Toyota Land Cruiser and a simple idea: travellers deserve a guide who knows the forest by name.

Our founder, Daniel Brooks, spent nine years as a ranger guide in Bwindi Impenetrable Forest and Queen Elizabeth National Park. He watched too many visitors rushed through on rigid group itineraries, never meeting the trackers, the porters or the communities who make a gorilla encounter possible.

So he did it differently. Small private groups. Unhurried days. Lodges that pay their staff fairly and give back to the land. Word spread — first among returning guests, then among their friends — and today our team of 34 plans journeys through Uganda, Rwanda, Kenya and Tanzania, still from our home on Kololo Hill in Kampala.

Milestones

Our journey so far

Steady, deliberate growth — never at the expense of the experience.

  1. 2012

    One car, big ambitions

    Daniel registers Brooks Tours in Kampala and leads every trip himself in a single Land Cruiser, mostly to Bwindi and Queen Elizabeth.

  2. 2014

    Into Rwanda

    We open a partner base in Musanze, adding Volcanoes National Park gorillas and golden monkeys to our cross-border itineraries.

  3. 2016

    Full AUTO membership

    Brooks becomes a full member of the Association of Uganda Tour Operators and expands to a fleet of six custom 4x4 safari vehicles.

  4. 2018

    Kenya & Tanzania

    Trusted ground partners in the Masai Mara and Serengeti let us offer seamless Great Migration safaris and Zanzibar beach extensions.

  5. 2020

    The 5% pledge

    Through the pandemic we kept every guide on payroll and committed 5% of annual profits to conservation and community projects — for good.

  6. 2024

    Fly-in & luxury collection

    We launch private fly-in gorilla safaris and a hand-picked collection of East Africa's finest lodges for honeymooners and milestone trips.

How we work

Four principles behind every trip

Private, never pooled

Every safari is yours alone — your own vehicle, your own guide, your own pace. No strangers, no fixed departures.

Guides who are experts

Our guides are UWA-trained and hold Uganda Safari Guides Association certification, with an average of 11 years in the field.

Honest, all-in pricing

Permits, park fees, lodges, meals and transfers are itemised up front. What we quote is what you pay — no surprises at the gate.

Travel that gives back

We favour community-owned lodges, hire locally and fund conservation with 5% of our profits, every single year.

Conservation & community

5% of our profits, pledged to the wild — every year

The gorillas, chimpanzees and lions you travel to see survive because local people choose to protect them. We make sure tourism makes that choice worthwhile.

Since 2020 we have committed five per cent of annual profits to conservation and community partners, on top of the park fees and permit revenue that already fund Uganda Wildlife Authority and Rwanda Development Board programmes.

  • Community lodges first: Buhoma Community Rest Camp, Nkuringo, and Lake Bunyonyi homestays
  • Gorilla conservation support via Conservation Through Public Health and the Bwindi trackers' welfare fund
  • Porter programmes that pay a fair daily wage to former poachers
  • Single-use plastic free vehicles and refill stations across our fleet
