Special offers 2026–2027

The same safaris, at a better price

Lodges release seasonal rates and we pass every dollar on. These are real savings on our most-loved private journeys, with no compromise on guides, vehicles or where you sleep.

How our offers work

Honest savings, never cut corners

Our offers come from three places: lodges filling quieter months, early-bird rates for high-season dates, and our own partners rewarding longer stays. The itinerary, private guide and Land Cruiser stay exactly the same.

  • Price shown is the price paid

    Permits, park fees and taxes are already inside the offer price.

  • Same lodges, same guides

    We never swap you into a cheaper camp to make an offer work.

  • Limited dates

    Gorilla permits sell out months ahead, so offers close when space runs out.

Beyond the headline offers

Last-minute & seasonal deals

Some of the best value never makes it onto this page because it appears and disappears within days. Here is where to look.

March–May & November

Green-season rates

The rains turn Uganda and Rwanda emerald, and many lodges cut rates by 15–30%. Gorilla treks run every day, birding is at its peak and you'll share the forest with far fewer people.

When to go →
Within 6 weeks of travel

Last-minute space

Camps release unsold rooms and the odd returned gorilla permit close to departure. If your dates are flexible, ask us and we'll check live availability across our partner lodges.

Check availability →
Families, couples, long stays

Stay-longer & family savings

Stay four nights and pay three at selected Serengeti and Zanzibar properties, honeymoon extras across Tanzania, and reduced rates for children under 12 sharing with parents.
