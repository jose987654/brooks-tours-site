Uganda passport services

Your East African e-passport, without the guesswork

Ugandan passports are applied for through the Ministry of Internal Affairs’ Directorate of Citizenship and Immigration Control (DCIC) online portal, then completed in person with biometric capture.

We fill in the online form, generate the URA payment reference (PRN), check every supporting document and book your capture date so you make one trip to the passport office, not three.

  • National ID (original and copy) or NIN slip
  • Birth certificate, or baptism card / school certificate for older applicants
  • Recommender’s letter and copy of their national ID (a recognised professional, e.g. lawyer, doctor, teacher or civil servant)
  • LC I letter from your village or zone of residence
  • Copies of parents’ national IDs (or death certificates)
  • Minors: both parents’ consent and IDs, and birth certificate
  • Bank payment receipt for the PRN
  • Your current or expired Ugandan passport (original)
  • National ID (original and copy)
  • Copy of the passport bio-data page
  • Bank payment receipt for the PRN
  • Name change: marriage certificate or deed poll
  • Police letter reporting the loss, with reference number
  • Sworn declaration (statutory declaration) on how the passport was lost
  • National ID (original and copy)
  • Copy of the lost passport’s bio-data page, if available
  • Damaged passport: the damaged book itself
  • Bank payment receipt for the PRN (replacement fee applies)

Booking a safari with us? We guide you through the e-visa for each country you visit, and check your application before you submit it.

UG

Uganda e-visa

$50 single entry

Valid 90 days. Apply online at the official immigration portal; approval usually takes 3–5 working days. Yellow fever certificate required.

EA

East Africa Tourist Visa

$100 multi-entry

One visa for Uganda, Rwanda and Kenya, valid 90 days. Apply through the country you enter first. Ideal for gorilla and Mara combos.

KE

Kenya eTA

approx. $30 per entry

Electronic Travel Authorisation for most visitors, applied for online at least 3 days before travel. Several African nationalities are exempt.

RW

Rwanda visa

$50 or visa-free

Visa on arrival or online for most nationalities; many African and Commonwealth citizens enter visa-free for 30 days.

Fees are indicative and set by each government. Always use official government portals; we never charge for information.

Questions

Passport & visa FAQ

Still unsure? Ask us on WhatsApp

Can you guarantee my visa will be approved?

No one honestly can. Visa decisions rest solely with the embassy or immigration authority. What we guarantee is a complete, accurate and well-presented application, which is the part you control. Our 98% figure reflects past files, not a promise.

How far ahead should I apply?

Start 6–8 weeks before travel for Schengen, UK and China, and 3–6 months ahead for the USA and Canada, where interview and processing queues are longest. Dubai and e-visas can be done in about a week.

Do I have to come to your office?

Not necessarily. Most clients send scans on WhatsApp or email and we review them remotely. You will still attend biometrics or an interview in person, and you are always welcome at our Kololo office.

What does your service fee cover?

Consultation, a personalised checklist, document review, form completion, appointment booking, a cover letter, flight and hotel reservations for your file and tracking until collection. Embassy, VFS and government fees are separate and paid at cost.

How much money should be on my bank statement?

There is no single figure. Embassies look for a stable income and enough funds to cover your trip comfortably, with no unexplained large deposits shortly before applying. We review your statements and advise before you submit.

My visa was refused before. Can you still help?

Yes. We read the refusal letter, address each reason in a new application and advise whether to reapply now, wait or appeal. Previous refusals must always be declared honestly.

How long does a Uganda passport take?

Typically around 10 working days after biometric capture, provided all documents are in order. Delays happen around peak periods, so apply well before any planned travel.

Does my passport need to be valid for six months?

For most countries, yes: at least six months beyond your return date, with two or more blank pages. If yours is close to expiry, renew it first; we can handle both together.

Is my personal information safe with you?

Your documents are used only for your application, stored securely and deleted on request after your trip. Original passports are signed in and out at our office.
