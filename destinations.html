Where we travel

Four countries. One wild heart.

From the misty volcanoes of the Virungas to the endless plains of the Serengeti and the spice-scented shores of Zanzibar — East Africa, known intimately by the people who call it home.

East Africa, up close

Every safari dream, a short hop apart

Uganda and Rwanda hold the world's last mountain gorillas. Kenya and Tanzania share the Great Migration. Zanzibar waits on the Indian Ocean for the end of the journey. Because the four countries sit side by side, one well-planned trip can combine them all — and our Kampala-based team designs, drives and guides every mile.

Multi-country safaris

Combine countries, multiply the wonder

The East Africa Tourist Visa ($100) covers Uganda, Rwanda and Kenya in one stamp, and short regional flights link every park. Three of our favourite pairings:

Rwanda + Uganda

Rwanda & Uganda Primate Combo

Track gorillas in both Volcanoes and Bwindi, chimpanzees in Nyungwe, and unwind on Lake Bunyonyi — two gorilla countries without a single flight.

Kenya + Uganda

Giraffe Manor to Gorillas

Breakfast with giraffes in Nairobi, big cats in a private Mara conservancy, then fly to Uganda for gorillas and chimps. Our signature luxury journey.

12 days · From $13,200 ppView trip →
Tanzania + Zanzibar

Serengeti & Zanzibar Honeymoon

The Serengeti and Ngorongoro Crater in intimate tented camps, followed by five barefoot days on Zanzibar's white-sand north coast.
