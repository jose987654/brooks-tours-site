01 · Visa assistance

Visa files prepared properly, the first time

Most refusals come down to paperwork: a missing bank stamp, a vague itinerary, an insurance policy that does not meet the rules. We make sure yours is complete before it goes in.

  • Destination-specific document checklists for your exact purpose of travel
  • Online forms completed and checked (DS-160, UK, Canada IRCC, Schengen)
  • VFS Global and embassy appointments booked in Kampala or Nairobi
  • Cover letters, flight reservations and hotel confirmations for your file
Visit our dedicated page Passport & Visa Assistance Country guides, checklist generator & online application →
02 · Uganda passports

New, renewed or replaced passports without the queues

Applications for the East African e-passport are made through the Directorate of Citizenship and Immigration Control (DCIC) online portal, then finished in person at Kololo or a regional office.

We complete the online form, generate your payment reference (PRN), check your recommender and LC letter, and book your biometric capture date so you only make the trip once.

Passport requirements →

03 · Air ticketing

Flights on any airline, out of Entebbe and beyond

From a single seat to Nairobi to forty delegates to Johannesburg, we search every carrier serving Entebbe International (EBB) and book the fare that fits your dates and baggage, not just the cheapest headline price.

  • All airlines: Uganda Airlines, Kenya Airways, RwandAir, Emirates, Qatar, Turkish, KLM, Ethiopian and more
  • Group, student and corporate fares with flexible name changes
  • Date changes, rebooking and refunds handled for you, not a call centre
  • Domestic and regional flights to Kihihi, Kisoro, Kidepo, Kigali and Zanzibar
04 · Hotels & lodges

Worldwide hotel and lodge booking

Business hotels in Nairobi and Dubai, family apartments in London, beach resorts in Zanzibar, tented camps in the Mara. We book at agency rates and confirm in writing, which embassies also accept as proof of accommodation.

  • Partner rates with safari lodges across Uganda, Rwanda, Kenya and Tanzania
  • Visa-ready hotel confirmations with refundable options
  • Conference and group room blocks in Kampala and Entebbe
05 · Transfers

Entebbe pickups and chauffeur cars, any hour

Your driver tracks your flight, waits in arrivals with a name board and has water and a local SIM ready. The Entebbe Expressway gets you to central Kampala in about 45 minutes.

  • 24/7 Entebbe airport meet-and-greet and drop-offs
  • Chauffeur-driven saloons, executive SUVs and 4x4 safari vehicles
  • Kampala city transport by the hour or day, and upcountry transfers
  • Fast-track and VIP lounge assistance on request
06 · Travel insurance

Travel and medical cover that embassies accept

We arrange policies with reputable underwriters for single trips, annual multi-trip and student travel, and we check the wording against your visa rules before you pay.

Inbound safari guests can add emergency evacuation cover, including AMREF Flying Doctors membership for remote parks.
